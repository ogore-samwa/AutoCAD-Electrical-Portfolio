# Optimizing Workspace Layouts and Tool Palette Interface Behaviors

Personal reference documentation and technical guide for managing floating window layouts, organizing software menus, and adjusting workspace interface properties to maximize drawing screen real estate in AutoCAD.

---

## Workspace Controls & Customization Workflows

### 1. Docking Tool Palettes Smoothly
* **The Concept:** Snapping an interface menu window directly to a perimeter edge of your application window so it integrates into your permanent workspace grid.
* **The Workflow:** Click and hold the palette's main title bar, drag it toward the left or right boundaries of your display screen until a placement layout outlines, and let go of your mouse click.
* **Overriding System Auto-Docking:** Hold down the **`Ctrl`** key while dragging a floating window around your display canvas to temporarily block it from snapping onto a window border automatically.
* **Sizing Adjustments:** Grab and pull the inside dividing seam of a snapped palette window to customize its display width.
* **Detaching Windows:** Left-click the title border, press and hold the **`Ctrl`** key, and pull the window out away from the border edge.

### 2. Utilizing Screen Anchors
* **The Concept:** Stowing a menu window flat against the boundary of your display workspace so it stays collapsed out of sight until you actively look for it.
* **The Workflow:** Right-click the palette's title banner (verify that **Allow Docking** is enabled first) and pick either **Anchor Left** or **Anchor Right**.
* **Interface Action:** The stowed menu stays tucked away as a small label tab. It will smoothly expand into full view the moment your cursor hovers over the tab border, and it folds right back up when your cursor leaves the area.
* **Stacking Configurations:** You can anchor several custom layout tools together onto the same side of your screen to have them arrange vertically.

### 3. Setting Up Auto-Hide Actions
* **The Concept:** An interactive layout style that collapses a tool window on the fly when it is not actively being used, keeping your vector drafting canvas clean.
* **The Workflow:** Click the small **Auto-Hide** icon button located near the top corner of the palette banner to toggle this behavior on or off.

### 4. Tuning Interface Transparency Levels
* **The Concept:** Changing the visual density or opacity of floating menus so you can see right through them to keep an eye on underlying project geometries.
* **The Workflow:** Right-click the palette's main title bar strip, choose **Transparency**, and dial in your preferred opacity percentages for both normal drawing states and cursor hover actions.
